# Deposit Frequently Asked Questions

## How long is the cooling-off period for a large-denomination CD?

Seven calendar days from the value date. No early withdrawal is accepted during this
period, and the branch must reject a request submitted on day 3 or earlier.

## Can a partial withdrawal be paid in cash?

No. Partial withdrawals are credited to the original funding account only. Cash
payment is not available for any CD withdrawal, including full early withdrawal.

## What is the minimum remaining balance after a partial withdrawal?

USD 100,000. A request that would leave a lower balance must instead be processed as a
full early withdrawal, and the depositor must be told the fee difference before signing.

## Is the early withdrawal fee negotiable?

A branch manager may waive up to 50% of the fee. Any waiver above 50% requires approval
from the Head of Retail Banking and must be documented in the case file.

## What interest is paid if the CD is withdrawn after 14 months?

The six-month tier rate for the balance tier applies pro rata to the days held. For a
USD 100,000 CD that rate is 2.80%, so 425 days of interest are paid at that rate.

## Does a rate decrease apply to an existing CD?

No. Rate changes apply to new bookings only. An existing CD keeps its contractual rate
until maturity, and Notice 2023-12 requires 30 days' notice before a decrease takes
effect for new bookings.

## What happens if the depositor dies?

The estate must submit a grant of probate and the beneficiary's identity document.
Legal and Compliance reviews the claim within 10 business days, and the early
withdrawal fee is waived for estate claims.

## Can a CD be transferred to another person?

Yes, with a notarized transfer form signed by both parties and branch manager approval.
The transferee must pass the standard KYC checks, and a pledged CD cannot be transferred
until the pledge is released.
