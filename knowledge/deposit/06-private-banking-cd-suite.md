# Private Banking CD Suite

## 1 Eligibility

The Private Banking CD Suite is available only to private banking clients with
investable assets above USD 10,000,000. Relationship managers must confirm the client
segment before quoting any rate.

## 2 Minimum Investment and Tenors

The minimum investment for the Private Banking CD Suite is USD 5,000,000 per
certificate. Tenors of 12, 24 and 36 months are available, and rates are quoted per
client by the private banking desk rather than from the retail catalogue.

## 3 Fees and Withdrawal Terms

No early withdrawal fee applies to the Private Banking CD Suite. A partial withdrawal
may not reduce the remaining balance below USD 5,000,000, and the client must give
10 business days' written notice for any withdrawal above USD 20 million.

## 4 Reporting and Review

Clients receive quarterly statements and an annual review with the relationship
manager. Suitability must be reconfirmed at each renewal, and the confidential rating
of this document must not be downgraded without Private Banking approval.
