# Legacy CD Terms 2024

## 1 Scope

These terms applied to certificates of deposit issued between 2024-01-01 and
2025-12-31. They are retained for audit and for the handful of 2024 CDs that have not
yet matured; new bookings must use `deposit-cd-001@3.2` instead.

## 2 Legacy Tier Rates

The 2024 schedule paid a single rate per tenor and applied it to the whole balance.
Tiering by balance was introduced only in the 2026 terms.

| Tenor | Legacy rate |
| --- | --- |
| 6 months | 3.80% |
| 12 months | 4.05% |
| 24 months | 4.20% |

## 3 Legacy Early Withdrawal Fee

The legacy fee was 2.00% of the withdrawn amount with no minimum charge. A legacy CD
that is withdrawn in 2026 keeps the legacy fee, because the fee schedule in force at
the value date applies for the life of the certificate.

## 4 Legacy Notice Requirements

No notice period applied to legacy CDs. The five-business-day notice introduced by
Circular [2022] No. 5 was implemented in the 2026 policy and does not apply
retroactively.
